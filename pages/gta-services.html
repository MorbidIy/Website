Security & Safety

Important Security Information

Modded accounts carry inherent risks. Rockstar Games actively monitors for modified accounts and may issue bans. We strongly recommend:

  • Using a secondary account for modded content
  • Enabling 2FA on your Rockstar Social Club
  • Never sharing login credentials
  • Reading our Account Security Guide thoroughly
