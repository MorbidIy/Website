Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a bug or issue with a mod?

Please create an issue on our GitHub repository with the following information: mod name, version, game version, steps to reproduce, and any error messages or logs.

Can I request a custom mod or feature?

Feature requests can be submitted on GitHub. Custom mod commissions are not currently accepted due to time constraints.

Are your mods compatible with other mods?

Most mods are compatible with each other, but conflicts can occur. Always test mods individually first. Check the Troubleshooting page for known conflicts.

Do you support console versions of Bully or GTA?

No, all mods and tools are for PC (Steam) versions only. Console modding is not supported.

Can I redistribute or repack your mods?

Please do not redistribute mods without permission. Link to the official download page instead. See Terms of Service for details.

How can I support your work?

Donations via Ko-fi are appreciated. Sharing mods with friends, reporting bugs, and contributing on GitHub also helps!
